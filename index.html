💡 Why Pakistani Women Choose ELÓRA™:

Designed with Ceramic Tourmaline Ionic Technology, this brush saturates airflow with negative ions to condition, smooth, and make hair shinier while reducing frizz and static. Say goodbye to heat damage and hello to effortless bouncy blowouts every morning!

Simple 4-Step Salon Blowout Routine

1
Towel Dry & Detangle

Towel dry hair to remove excess water, then run a wide-tooth comb through damp hair to detangle for the smoothest styling results.

2
Section Your Hair

Separate hair into manageable upper and lower sections using styling clips so you can style layer by layer effortlessly.

3
Choose Heat Setting

Use the LOW setting on fine, thin, or damaged hair, and the HIGH setting on thick, coarse, or curly hair.

4
Style & Volumize

Place the brush close to the roots and brush down toward the ends. For extra crown volume, hold the brush under the roots for 3 seconds.
